Cross Camp: Extreme — Roles Edition

A two-player strategy game. Each player controls an army moving along two routes between bases at opposite corners of a square.

Components and setup

  • A square board with two routes. Each route has 12 rows, two tracks per row: 48 field spaces total. Each half is numbered 1–6 outward from its base; the 6s meet at the bends.
  • Per player: 6 Agents (●), 2 Guards (◆), 2 Runners (➤), 5 base-health tokens and 2 repair tokens.
  • Three ordinary six-sided dice. Use small markers to show which pieces have spent a movement die and which attacks are prepared.
  • All pieces start at home. Ivory takes the first turn. All information is public.

Piece roles

PieceAbilityLimitation
Agent ●Uses all normal movement, capture, attack and base-scoring rules.No special ability.
Guard ◆A full friendly pair containing a Guard cannot be vaulted.Cannot enter the enemy base.
Runner ➤A movement die advances it any distance from 1 up to the die value.Cannot capture, strike or lead an assault. It can support an Agent or Guard.

Each piece falls to one successful capture. Pieces do not have individual health. Any piece at home can repair the base.

Your turn

  1. Roll all three dice once.
  2. Choose a remaining die. Spend it for movement, its printed action, a coordinated attack, or to finish a ready attack. You may instead skip that die.
  3. Continue in any order until all dice are spent or skipped. You may end the turn early and forfeit the rest.
  4. Clear your movement markers. Your opponent takes a full three-die turn.

An individual piece may use one movement die per turn. It can still receive actions before or afterward. Actions that relocate pieces, such as switching routes, returning home or Vault, do not spend a movement die. A reverse combination uses one action die and one movement die. Never add two movement dice together for one piece.

Ordinary movement

Agents and Guards advance exactly the movement die’s value in rows. Runners may advance 1 up to that value. Choose either route when deploying; afterward, stay on that route unless an action changes it. Within a route, either track is available at each destination, so you can weave around single pieces. You cannot land on a friendly piece.

Land on a lone enemy to capture it, unless your moving piece is a Runner. Jumping a lone enemy does not capture it. A full row blocks ordinary passage for both teams, even if it contains one piece from each team. An enemy friendly pair also prevents ordinary capture landings on either piece.

Die actions

DieActionEffect
1ReverseStep back one row as an action, or combine the 1 with a different unused die to go backward exactly that die’s value. The combination spends both dice and is that piece’s one movement die. Obey walls and normal landing restrictions.
2Change routesSwitch to either track of the other route at the same distance from base. You may capture a lone enemy, but never land on a protected pair. Runners cannot capture.
3Strike AheadAn Agent or Guard attacks either enemy in the row immediately ahead without moving. Lone enemies and pairs invading your turf are captured immediately. A pair on its own turf gets the response described below.
4Return HomeRecall one field piece to your base without capturing.
5VaultAdvance 1–5 rows as an action, jumping ordinary walls. You cannot cross a full friendly pair containing a Guard, land on a protected enemy pair, or land on a friendly piece. Agents and Guards may capture a lone enemy; Runners cannot. Guards cannot enter the enemy base.
6RepairA piece at home spends this die and one repair token to restore one base health, up to five. Each side can repair only twice per game. At full health, repair is unavailable and costs nothing.

Blockades and home turf

Two friendly pieces side by side form a blockade. If either is a Guard, Vault cannot cross it. Other blockades can be vaulted, but cannot be captured by a Vault landing or route switch.

To make a coordinated attack, place two of your pieces side by side in the row immediately before an enemy pair. Spend any die. An Agent or Guard leads; any friendly piece, including a Runner, may support.

  • Enemy pair on your turf: capture one blocker immediately. The leader moves into its space; the support stays.
  • Enemy pair defending its own turf: spend one die to prepare. Nobody moves or is captured. The defender takes its full turn. If all involved pieces remain, spend any die on your next turn to finish: capture the chosen blocker and move the leader into its space.
  • Strike Ahead against a home-defending pair: a 3 prepares the strike; after the same full response, any die can finish it. The striker stays in place.

Mark the target and attacker when preparing. Moving either blocker, moving any involved attacker or support, or capturing an involved attacker cancels preparation immediately. Extra dice cannot finish an attack before the opponent’s full response. Once ready, an attack can be finished later while all required pieces remain.

Base attacks and winning

Enter the enemy base on an exact movement distance to deal one damage, then immediately return that piece home. Defenders in the base do not prevent the damage. Guards cannot enter it; Agents and Runners can. If the piece spent a movement die before returning, it still cannot use another movement die that turn.

Win immediately when the rival base reaches zero health or the rival has no pieces left. Victory ends the game even if dice remain. In the digital version, an unfinished game draws after 160 player turns (80 rounds); for a physical game, use an 80-round track if you want the same limit.

Tabletop reminders

Use shape or symbols as well as color for roles. Put a small marker beside a piece after it spends its movement die. Place used dice aside, remove health and repair tokens as spent, and use an arrow marker for a prepared attack. Keep these rules beside the board; no app is required.
