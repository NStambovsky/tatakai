THE BRIEFING

A small board.
A quiet threat.

Both players begin with six agents in their home camp. You play Ivory and move first.

  1. Roll, then relocate. A roll of 1–5 lets you move one agent to an empty space with that number. Agents leaving camp must deploy to their own side first. On a later roll, an agent on the battlefield may cross to the rival side. The roll is a destination number, not a number of steps.
  2. Capture or cover. On either side, rolling a row’s number lets you move into the empty space beside an enemy. This does not capture them. They get a response turn to move away, countercapture if eligible, or skip. If both agents stay, on your next turn you may capture in place without another matching roll; that capture uses your whole turn. A home agent responding to an approaching invader still needs the row’s number to countercapture. Use the Capture enemy piece button. If several captures are available, select your agent or choose a target from the list. Red outlines mark a face-off, not an automatic capture; the button explains when a matching roll is needed. Camp defence on sixes is unchanged. Two friendly agents occupying both spaces in the same numbered row protect each other: there is no empty partner space for an enemy to enter.
  3. Six changes everything. On any turn, before or after rolling any number, you may choose Skip turn and leave every agent in place, even when a move or capture is available. Skipping uses your turn. On a 6, any battlefield agent may return to your camp. Only an agent already on the rival’s side may enter their camp. You cannot jump directly from your home camp to the rival camp. Return home to capture one enemy infiltrator, enter the rival camp and wait, or use an agent already at home to remove an infiltrator without moving. To do that, select your camp and tap it again. Entering their camp does not capture its defenders.
  4. Leave no opening. You win as soon as you have an agent in the enemy camp and no enemy defenders remain there. Watch what happens when your last defender leaves!

Ultra mode

Choose Ultra when starting a new game. Both sides start with 3 coins. A capture earns 3 coins. End your own turn with at least one agent in the enemy camp to earn 1 coin, including a skipped turn. Multiple infiltrators do not multiply income. Coins last for this game only.

Buy at most one power per turn: Reroll (2) or Set your roll (4) after rolling; Set enemy’s next roll (5) before or after rolling; or Recruit agent (6) at home, up to eight living agents. Recruitment uses your turn and does not capture an infiltrator. Other powers leave your move or skip available. An enemy-set roll is locked: no reroll or replacement that turn. A queued roll remains until the player actually rolls. Both sides see queued rolls and use the same prices and limits. Classic has no coins or powers.

Three ways to play the computer

Easy chooses freely among legal moves. Medium weighs captures, protection, and camp safety. Hard also considers your best reply for each possible dice roll. All levels use the same fair die.

Browser edition conventions

The creator’s videos do not cover every edge case. In this edition: agents leaving camp deploy to their home side; battlefield agents can relocate to empty matching numbered spaces on either side; home-side capture advantage applies symmetrically; each numbered space holds one agent; agents paired across the same row protect each other; captured agents stay out; a returning or resident defender removes one infiltrator; a turn with no legal move is passed; losing all agents loses the game; a game is drawn after 300 moves. These are stated implementation choices, not a claim to reproduce the complete printed rulebook.

Tap or click an occupied space to select an agent, then a highlighted destination. Keyboard users can Tab between spaces and press Enter. Progress saves automatically; starting a new game replaces it.
