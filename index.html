THE BRIEFING

A small board.
A quiet threat.

Both players begin with six agents in their home camp. You play Ivory and move first.

  1. Roll, then relocate. A roll of 1–5 lets you move one agent to any open space with that number, anywhere on the board. The roll is a destination number, not a number of steps.
  2. Capture or cover. Landing on a lone enemy removes it. Two friendly agents occupying both spaces in the same numbered row protect each other: an enemy cannot land there.
  3. Six changes everything. On a 6, move one agent to either camp. Return home to capture one enemy infiltrator, or enter the rival camp and wait. Entering their camp does not capture its defenders.
  4. Leave no opening. You win as soon as you have an agent in the enemy camp and no enemy defenders remain there. Watch what happens when your last defender leaves!

Three ways to play the computer

Easy chooses freely among legal moves. Medium weighs captures, protection, and camp safety. Hard also considers your best reply for each possible dice roll. All levels use the same fair die.

Browser edition conventions

The creator’s short video does not cover every edge case. In this edition: any surviving agent can relocate to any matching numbered space; each numbered space holds one agent; agents paired across the same row protect each other; captured agents stay out; a returning defender removes one infiltrator; a turn with no legal move is passed; losing all agents loses the game; a game is drawn after 300 moves. These are stated implementation choices, not a claim to reproduce the complete printed rulebook.

Tap or click an occupied space to select an agent, then a highlighted destination. Keyboard users can Tab between spaces and press Enter. Progress saves automatically; starting a new game replaces it.
